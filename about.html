About Me

I am a Forest Engineer and tropical forest researcher specializing in Amazonian forest ecology, forest dynamics, and sustainable forest management.

My research focuses on understanding how environmental gradients, ecological processes, and management practices influence tropical forest structure, biodiversity, biomass accumulation, and carbon storage.

Current Position

Postdoctoral Researcher
Programa de Pesquisa Ecológica de Longa Duração (PELD)
Universidade do Estado do Amapá

Research Interests

🌳 Tropical forest ecology and dynamics

🌲 Giant trees and forest structure

🌿 Aboveground biomass and carbon assessment

🛰 Remote sensing and airborne LiDAR applications

🌱 Sustainable forest management and adaptive management strategies

Research Approach

I integrate permanent forest inventories, remote sensing technologies, airborne LiDAR data, ecological modelling, and field-based observations to investigate tropical forest functioning and develop approaches that support forest conservation and sustainable use.

Amazonian Forests

My work contributes to improving our understanding of Amazonian forests by combining ecological knowledge with innovative technologies to evaluate forest resilience, carbon dynamics, biodiversity patterns, and sustainable management opportunities.
