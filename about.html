About Me

I am a Forest Scientist specializing in tropical forest management, ecology, and conservation, with a strong academic background in Forest Engineering and Forest Sciences. My research focuses on understanding the dynamics and functioning of tropical forests through long-term monitoring, permanent forest plots, ecological modelling, and integrated approaches combining field data and remote sensing technologies.

I have experience in long-term forest monitoring networks, forest dynamics, aboveground biomass and carbon estimation, ecological modelling, and applied research in Amazonian and Andean tropical forests. My work involves field research, coordination of forest inventories, data analysis, and collaboration with national and international research institutions in Brazil and Ecuador.

Research Approach

My research integrates permanent forest inventories, remote sensing, airborne LiDAR, satellite products, and ecological modelling to investigate tropical forest structure, biodiversity patterns, carbon dynamics, and ecosystem responses to environmental changes.

Through interdisciplinary approaches and international collaborations, my goal is to generate scientific knowledge that supports sustainable forest management, conservation strategies, and improved understanding of tropical forests under global change.
