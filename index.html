Welcome

I am a Forest Engineer and tropical forest researcher working on the ecology, dynamics, and sustainable management of Amazonian forests. My research focuses on understanding how environmental gradients, forest processes, and human interventions influence forest structure, biodiversity, biomass accumulation, and carbon storage.

My work integrates long-term forest inventories, airborne LiDAR, satellite remote sensing, ecological modelling, and forest management approaches to improve our understanding of tropical forest resilience and sustainable use.

Research Areas

🌳 Giant trees and forest structure in tropical forests

🌎 Long-term ecological monitoring and forest dynamics

🌿 Aboveground biomass, carbon stocks, and allometric modelling

🛰 LiDAR and remote sensing for forest assessment

🌱 Sustainable forest management and adaptive management strategies

🌲 Tropical forest resilience under environmental change

Current Research

My current research investigates Amazonian forests through integrated field monitoring and remote sensing approaches, with emphasis on giant trees, carbon dynamics, biodiversity conservation, and sustainable forest management.
