Research Regions and Study Sites

My research is conducted across tropical forests in South America, with current activities concentrated in Brazil and Ecuador. These study regions represent contrasting ecological contexts, including protected forests, managed forests, and long-term ecological monitoring sites.

Brazilian Amazon

Research activities in Brazil include forest ecosystems in the states of Amapá and Pará, encompassing protected areas, giant tree monitoring sites, and managed tropical forests.

  • Parque Nacional Montanhas do Tumucumaque (Amapá): long-term ecological monitoring, giant trees, forest structure, biomass, and carbon dynamics.
  • Estação Ecológica do Jari (Amapá/Pará region): forest inventories, biodiversity studies, biomass assessment, and ecological monitoring.
  • Amazonian managed forests (Pará): sustainable forest management research, permanent plots, forest recovery, growth, mortality, and recruitment dynamics.

Ecuadorian Amazon

Current collaborations in Ecuador focus on strengthening international research networks for tropical forest monitoring, biodiversity assessment, and sustainable forest management.

  • Forest monitoring initiatives developed through collaborations with Ecuadorian research institutions.
  • Integration of long-term ecological data and knowledge exchange between Amazonian countries.
  • Development of comparative approaches for understanding tropical forest dynamics across regions.
